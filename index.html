How we work

Is this a fit?

We’re a good fit if

  • You care about your work and how it’s shared.
  • You’re at any stage: a first idea, a project underway, or something ready to share.
  • You want an ongoing partner, or support for one project.
  • You need one piece of work or one creative role, without handing over the whole project.
  • You want to work together, with your ideas shaping every step.

We may not be the right fit if

  • You want someone to follow a brief without any back and forth.
  • Getting it done fast matters more than getting it right.
